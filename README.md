# ⚽ Matchday — Squad Stats & Leaderboard

A live football (soccer) stats tracker for your squad. Log your games, get ratings, and compete on a shared leaderboard.

## Features

✅ **Register with username & position** — no email/password friction
✅ **Log games instantly** — quick-add stat buttons (+1, +2, +3) for fast entry
✅ **Position-based stats** — different fields for Goalkeepers, Defenders, Midfielders, Forwards
✅ **Auto-calculated ratings** — each game gets a 1–10 rating based on your performance
✅ **Live leaderboard** — ranked by average rating, updated in real-time
✅ **Player of the Week badge 🏆** — highest rated player this week gets POW
✅ **Game history** — see all your past matches with stats and ratings
✅ **Weekly reveal system** — games stay private until Monday, then show on leaderboard

## How It Works

1. **Open the app** → Share the link with your squad
2. **Join** → Pick a username and position
3. **Log a game** → Fill stats or use quick-add buttons
4. **Get a rating** → App calculates 1–10 based on your position's formula
5. **Check leaderboard** → See how you stack up against teammates
6. **Monday reveal** → All game
